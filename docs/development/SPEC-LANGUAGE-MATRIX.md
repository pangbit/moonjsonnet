## Completed named-rule reviews

These are recorded manual decisions from constructor case analysis plus executed controls. The tool checks empty premises for premise-free judgments, or exact fixed statements and premise roles for the explicitly supported conditional, Boolean shortcut, sequence-length, object-reflection, object-head, chk-error and chk-unary transfer judgments. The sequence form is restricted to length-array/string, pins element/codepoint units, and excludes the array-concat formal index discrepancy A0. The Boolean form is restricted to the two explicit shortcut rules and excludes the longcut non-Boolean RHS domain B0. The reflection form is restricted to object-has-ex/fields-ex, pins Object/String/Boolean domains, exact name membership or sorted unique name output, excludes payload demand and retains N0/R0/A0/B0 boundaries. The object-head form is restricted to length-object/type-object, preserves the raw length delimiter blemish and explicit composition interpretation, checks the fixed public field-count source and completed dependencies, and excludes nested value/assertion demand and N0/R0. The comparison-sign form pins the six unambiguous <, <= and >= equations, ordered operands and admitted cmp signs -1/0/1; it excludes greater-than formulas and whole comparator child judgments. The Boolean-negation form pins only not-true/not-false, their strict Boolean operand and opposite result, ! and operand-head-only demand; it assumes the operand child and excludes other unary and whole static/grammar judgments. The static-pair whitelist reuses the fixed-statement/premise-role checks only for chk-array-index and chk-binary, preserving target/index or left/right roles and assuming the unchanged-Gamma child judgments; runtime type/integer/range and operator evaluation obligations remain separate. The value-head entry pins the exact Primitive/Function/Array membership and identity equation; it requires canonical finite members and explicit delayed-child scope, excluding raw numeric conversion, Object construction and manifestation. The unary record also pins the four-symbol grammar domain. It checks inspected implementation/test hashes and explicit scope; source/name matches do not prove semantic correctness. Transfer judgments assume their child judgments and do not complete other language rules or whole-language acceptance.
